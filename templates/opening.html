FINANCE SETUP

Opening balances & historical uploads

This version uses 31 October 2026 as the member contribution opening position and 30 October 2026 as the individual loan snapshot date. Upload individual outstanding loans separately so each repayment deadline can be tracked.
1. Upload member balances as at 31 October 2026

Enter SHARE, SOCIAL, unpaid penalties and other member balances. Do not enter aggregate loan principal or interest here; each outstanding loan must be uploaded separately below.

Download opening balance template
Enter the actual wallet balance at the opening date. This is the starting cash balance for all subsequent M-Koba movements.
2. Import ALL historical loans as at 30 October 2026

Include every loan: active, partly paid and fully repaid/closed. One row per loan. Enter total amount paid and, where available, split it into interest collected and principal repaid. The system records interest income and principal recovery separately and tracks remaining instalment deadlines.

Download all-loans import template
Partial instalment payments are accepted. Any unusual instalment count, amount or closed-loan balance is recorded as a finding and held for Administrator approval before posting. Fully paid loans are retained with Closed status. Complete the optional Repayment History sheet with actual receipt dates and M-Koba transaction IDs for transaction-level matching. If that history is unavailable, cumulative paid amounts are recorded as at 30 October 2026.
3. Upload transactions after 31 October 2026

Use this for contributions, new loans and repayments dated from 1 November 2026 onwards. Upload contributions and loans before their related repayments.
