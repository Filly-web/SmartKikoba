Opening balances & historical uploads

1. Upload member balances as at 31 October 2026

Enter SHARE, SOCIAL, unpaid penalties and other member balances. Do not enter aggregate loan principal or interest here; each outstanding loan must be uploaded separately below.

2. Upload outstanding loans as at 30 October 2026

One row per loan. Record original amount, total instalments, number of instalments fully paid and cumulative amount received up to 30 October. The system creates the remaining instalment schedule and due dates.

Download outstanding loans template
The amount paid must agree with the number of complete instalments reported. Any extra partial payment is allocated to the next instalment.
