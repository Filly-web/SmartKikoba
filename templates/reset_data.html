Clear group data

Permanent and destructive action. This will delete all member records, contributions, loan records and instalment schedules, opening balances, cash movements, M-Pesa reconciliations and contribution periods from the connected database. This cannot be undone from the portal.
What will be retained?

What will happen to other users?

Non-administrator accounts will be deactivated and unlinked from member records. You can create fresh member and user records after the reset.
